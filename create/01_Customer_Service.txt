MOST RELEVANT Q&A

Q: Can Operava provide only one customer service staff member? A: Yes. A
business can engage one dedicated professional for a specific workload
or customer channel. The service can also expand into a team or multiple
specialized teams.

Q: Can Operava support multiple customer service channels? A: Yes.
Depending on the engagement, professionals can support voice, email,
live chat, tickets, social inquiries, order concerns, account questions,
and escalation workflows.

Q: Is this only for large companies? A: No. The model is designed for
small businesses, growing companies, established organizations, and
enterprises. The staffing level can be matched to actual operational
demand.

Q: Can the staff follow our existing processes? A: Yes. Operava can
align assigned professionals with approved procedures, scripts,
knowledge bases, escalation rules, service levels, and client-specific
tools.

Q: What is the main business benefit? A: It provides access to skilled
operational talent while reducing the need to build and manage every
support function entirely in-house.

Q: Can the team scale? A: Yes. Staffing can be structured around one
role, one team, or multiple teams, allowing support capacity to grow
with customer volume.

OPERAVA SERVICE MESSAGE Your customers should experience your brand—not
