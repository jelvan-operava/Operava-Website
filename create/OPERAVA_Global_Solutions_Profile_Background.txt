OPERAVA GLOBAL SOLUTIONS COMPANY PROFILE & BUSINESS BACKGROUND

Operating in Advance. Technology, Workforce & Business Process
Outsourcing — Connected Remotely and Globally.

1.  COMPANY OVERVIEW

OPERAVA Global Solutions is a Philippine-based technology, workforce,
and business process solutions company focused on helping organizations
operate more efficiently through technology, skilled professionals, and
digitally enabled services.

OPERAVA combines Information Technology services with Business Process
Outsourcing (BPO), remote workforce solutions, and operational support
to help businesses build, modernize, connect, and operate their digital
and business processes.

The company is designed around a simple principle:

MAKE WORK AND SERVICES ACCESSIBLE — ANYTIME, ANYWHERE.

OPERAVA believes that modern businesses should not be limited by
physical location when technology and skilled people can connect
organizations, customers, employees, and business processes remotely.

Our objective is to make high-quality technology, professional services,
and skilled remote talent more accessible to startups, small businesses,
growing companies, established organizations, and enterprises.
