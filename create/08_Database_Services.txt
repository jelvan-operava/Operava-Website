MOST RELEVANT Q&A

Q: What database services can Operava provide? A: Depending on the
engagement, services can include database design, administration,
maintenance, optimization, monitoring, migration support, data
organization, troubleshooting, and development support.

Q: Can Operava work with an existing database? A: Yes. Existing
environments can be assessed and supported according to the available
architecture, access, technology, and business requirements.

Q: Can database services support application development? A: Yes.
Database professionals can work with development teams on data models,
queries, application integration, performance, and related technical
requirements.

Q: Can Operava help with database migration? A: Yes. Migration support
can include planning, data preparation, transfer, validation, testing,
and post-migration activities within the agreed scope.

Q: Can database support be provided by one specialist? A: Yes. One
database professional can support a defined workload, while larger
environments can be supported by specialized teams.
