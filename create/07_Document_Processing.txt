MOST RELEVANT Q&A

Q: What is document processing? A: It is the structured handling of
business documents, which may include intake, sorting, classification,
indexing, data extraction, verification, updating, and records
organization.

Q: Can Operava process digital documents? A: Yes. Digital document
workflows can be supported where the client provides appropriate
systems, access, procedures, and document formats.

Q: Can paper-based documents be part of a process? A: Potentially,
depending on the agreed operational setup, document availability,
scanning requirements, security controls, and workflow.

Q: Can document processing include data extraction? A: Yes.
Professionals can extract defined information from documents and enter
or organize it in approved systems.

Q: Can sensitive business documents be processed? A: Potentially,
subject to the engagement’s confidentiality, access controls, security
measures, privacy requirements, and applicable laws.
