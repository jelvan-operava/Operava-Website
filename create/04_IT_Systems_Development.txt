MOST RELEVANT Q&A

Q: What is an IT business system? A: It is a technology solution
designed to support specific organizational processes, such as
operations, records, approvals, reporting, workforce management,
customer processes, or internal administration.

Q: Can Operava automate our existing workflow? A: Yes. Existing
processes can be reviewed and translated into digital workflows,
business rules, interfaces, notifications, approvals, and system
functions.

Q: Can multiple departments use the same system? A: Yes. Systems can be
designed around shared workflows, permissions, roles, departments, and
data requirements.

Q: Can an IT system integrate with existing software? A: Yes.
Integration can be included where appropriate through APIs, databases,
connectors, or other supported mechanisms.

Q: Can the system grow with our company? A: The architecture can be
planned around expected users, workflows, data, integrations, and future
requirements.

OPERAVA SERVICE MESSAGE Operational complexity should not prevent
