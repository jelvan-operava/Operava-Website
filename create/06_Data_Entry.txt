MOST RELEVANT Q&A

Q: What can data-entry professionals handle? A: Depending on the
workflow, they can enter, update, transfer, format, verify, classify, or
maintain information in approved systems and documents.

Q: Is data entry only for spreadsheets? A: No. Work can involve
spreadsheets, databases, CRM systems, ERP systems, forms, records,
catalogs, portals, and other authorized business platforms.

Q: Can Operava handle repetitive high-volume work? A: Yes. Team-based
staffing can be designed for sustained or seasonal data-entry workloads.

Q: How is quality supported? A: The workflow can include standardized
instructions, validation procedures, review steps, productivity
monitoring, and quality checks according to the agreed requirements.

Q: Can one person be assigned to us? A: Yes. One dedicated professional
can be assigned to a defined workload, with the option to scale into
additional staff or teams.
