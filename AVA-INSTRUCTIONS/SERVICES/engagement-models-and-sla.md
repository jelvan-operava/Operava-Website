# Engagement Models, Pricing Structure & Service Level Agreements

## Engagement Models

### 1. Dedicated Teams & Squads (Most Popular)
- **Structure**: Full-time dedicated engineers, support specialists, or operations analysts assigned 100% to your account.
- **Management**: You maintain direct day-to-day task management, while OPERAVA handles payroll, hardware, benefits, HR, and infrastructure.
- **Billing**: Simple, predictable monthly seat rate with no hidden fees.

### 2. Project-Based Delivery
- **Structure**: Fixed-scope, fixed-price delivery with defined milestones and sprint deliverables.
- **Best For**: Web/mobile application builds, infrastructure migrations, cloud audits, or security assessments.
- **Billing**: Milestone-based payments tied to verified client sign-offs.

### 3. Staff Augmentation
- **Structure**: Individual specialists embedded directly into existing client teams to bridge immediate skill gaps.
- **Speed**: Candidate shortlists delivered within 48 hours; onboarding completed in 7–14 days.

## Service Level Agreements (SLA)
- **Infrastructure Uptime**: 99.99% availability guarantee.
- **24/7 Incident Response**:
  - Severity 1 (Critical): < 15 minute initial response, 2-hour target resolution.
  - Severity 2 (Major): < 30 minute initial response, 4-hour target resolution.
  - Severity 3 (Minor): < 2 hour response.
- **BPO Customer Support**:
  - Live chat first response: < 60 seconds.
  - Email ticketing first response: < 30 minutes.
  - Target CSAT: > 95%.
