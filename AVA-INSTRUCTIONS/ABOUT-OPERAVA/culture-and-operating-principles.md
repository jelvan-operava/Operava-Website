# Culture & Operating Principles — OPERAVA Global Solutions

## Core Operating Principles
1. **Operate in Advance**: Proactive problem solving instead of reactive troubleshooting. We anticipate bottlenecks before they impact production.
2. **Craftsmanship & Quality**: High engineering standards, rigorous peer reviews, and strict QA automation across all client deliverables.
3. **Radical Transparency**: Clear reporting, open communication channels, and real-time operational visibility for every client engagement.
4. **People-First Growth**: Continuous learning stipends, modern tooling, and empathetic leadership for our global workforce.

## Working Philosophy
- **Remote-First with Regional Hubs**: Our teams collaborate smoothly across multiple time zones (US, APAC, EMEA) using asynchronous best practices.
- **Dedicated Squads**: Team members are embedded directly into client workflows and culture rather than treated as transactional vendors.
- **Security by Default**: Every team member operates on encrypted devices with multi-factor authentication, endpoint detection, and strict access controls.
