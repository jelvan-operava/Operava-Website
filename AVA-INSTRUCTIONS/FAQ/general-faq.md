# Frequently Asked Questions — OPERAVA Global Solutions

## General Questions

### Where is OPERAVA located?
OPERAVA is initially registered at Pagudpud, Ilocos Norte 2919, Philippines. We operate digitally and globally, servicing clients across North America, Europe, Australia, and Asia-Pacific through a remote-first, distributed delivery model.

### How quickly can a dedicated team start?
For standard engineering and BPO roles, we provide pre-vetted candidate shortlists within 48 to 72 hours. Complete onboarding and kickoff typically take 7 to 14 business days.

### What security certifications does OPERAVA hold?
We maintain ISO 27001 certification, SOC 2 Type II compliance, and strict adherence to HIPAA and GDPR regulations. All client data is encrypted at rest and in transit.

### How does billing and invoicing work?
We offer transparent monthly invoicing for dedicated team members with no setup fees or hidden recruitment charges. For project-based work, invoices are tied to agreed milestone deliverables.

### Can we interview candidates before hiring?
Yes. You participate directly in candidate interviews and technical reviews. You have final approval on every team member placed on your account.

### How do I apply for a job or get a project quote?
You can apply directly through our Careers page or submit an inquiry through our Contact page. You can also ask AVA right here in this chat to submit your details directly to our team.
