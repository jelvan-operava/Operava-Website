# Hiring & Interview Process — OPERAVA Careers

## Recruitment Flow

### 1. Application Submission
- **Action**: Candidate submits resume and portfolio via our Careers page or through AVA.
- **Timeline**: Reviewed within 24 to 48 business hours.

### 2. Initial Talent Screening (30 minutes)
- **Format**: Video call with our Talent Acquisition team.
- **Focus**: Background review, communication skills, career goals, work arrangement preferences, and salary expectations.

### 3. Technical Assessment or Practical Challenge
- **Engineering Roles**: Hands-on coding challenge or architectural review.
- **Support & Operations**: Scenario-based customer handling or problem-solving test.
- **Security Roles**: Practical incident triage or vulnerability review.

### 4. Technical / Team Interview (45–60 minutes)
- **Format**: In-depth discussion with the Lead Engineer or Department Manager.
- **Focus**: System design, past project experience, problem-solving approach, and team collaboration.

### 5. Final Cultural & Leadership Discussion
- **Format**: Brief conversation with an OPERAVA director or client partner (for dedicated squads).
- **Focus**: Culture fit, onboarding schedule, and expectations.

### 6. Official Offer & Fast-Track Onboarding
- **Action**: Formal offer letter with complete benefits summary.
- **Onboarding**: Hardware provisioning, security orientation, team introduction, and assigned buddy within week one.
