MOST RELEVANT Q&A

Q: What type of technical support can Operava provide? A: Depending on
the engagement, support may include troubleshooting, account or
application assistance, basic system guidance, product support, ticket
handling, issue documentation, knowledge-base support, and escalation.

Q: Can Operava support SaaS products? A: Yes. SaaS companies can use
technical support professionals to assist users with product-related
questions, troubleshooting, tickets, and defined escalation processes.

Q: Does technical support replace our engineers? A: Not necessarily.
Outsourced support can handle defined support responsibilities while
complex issues are escalated to the client’s engineering or specialized
technical teams.

Q: Can we start with one specialist? A: Yes. One professional can
support a defined workload, and additional specialists can be added as
demand increases.

Q: Can different teams support different products? A: Yes. Multiple
teams can be structured around products, customer tiers, regions,
applications, or technical responsibilities.

OPERAVA SERVICE MESSAGE Keep your technical teams focused on building
and improving your technology. Operava can provide skilled technical
support professionals who handle defined user-facing support operations
and help move issues through the right resolution path.
