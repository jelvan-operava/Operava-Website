==================================================

1. SYSTEM PURPOSE
   ==================================================

OPERAVA Recruitment AVA is an AI-powered recruitment application designed as a ChatGPT-style conversational experience.

The applicant should not navigate through multiple traditional forms.

The entire application process is conducted through one primary interface:

LEFT PANEL:
Live Applicant Profile

RIGHT PANEL:
Recruitment AVA Chat

AVA identity:

RECRUITMENT AVA
Your Virtual Assistant for today

AVA guides the applicant through the application, asks required questions, acknowledges answers professionally and in a friendly manner, identifies missing information, gathers structured information, and automatically updates the applicant profile.

Once all required application information has been gathered and validated, AVA creates the applicant profile and prepares the applicant for a 30-question assessment.

The entire system must be connected to real production services.

No demo records.
No mock API.
No fake applicant.
No simulated assessment.
No fake score.
No placeholder backend.
No fake email success message.

If a production dependency is unavailable, the system must display an actual error or controlled unavailable state rather than pretending the operation succeeded.

|
v

|
v
Send verification email
|
v
Applicant enters OTP
|
v
Worker validates OTP
|
v
Email verified
|
v
Persistent applicant information may now be saved

OTP REQUIREMENTS:

- six-digit code
- short expiration period
- one-time use
- previous active OTP invalidated when a new OTP is issued
- resend rate limiting
- verification attempt rate limiting
- secure OTP storage
- plaintext OTP must not be logged
- OTP must not be returned through the frontend API
- excessive failed attempts must temporarily lock verification

This should follow the same security principle as the existing OPERAVA form-submission email verification logic, specifically adapted for Application Email Verification.

9. AVA CONVERSATION ENGINE

AVA must behave as a professional and friendly recruitment assistant.

AVA should not behave like an uncontrolled general-purpose chatbot.

AVA should:

- understand the current application category
- ask the next required question
- recognize complete answers
- detect incomplete answers
- ask follow-up questions
- acknowledge answers
- extract structured information
- update the applicant profile
- continue the application flow
- avoid asking questions that have already been answered
- avoid unnecessarily repeating information
- maintain conversation context
- stay within Recruitment AVA's authorized knowledge

Example:

AVA:

"How many years of customer service experience do you have?"

Applicant:

"I worked in customer service for three years."

AVA:

"Thank you. I've recorded three years of customer service experience. What type of customer interactions did you primarily handle?"

The profile can update immediately:

EXPERIENCE
3 years

28. COPY / TEXT RESTRICTIONS

The assessment interface should discourage normal copy/paste behavior.

Controls can include:

- disable normal text selection
- disable context menu
- disable Ctrl/Cmd+C
- disable Ctrl/Cmd+X
- disable Ctrl/Cmd+V where appropriate
- disable text dragging
- restrict copying from assessment content

These controls are not absolute security.

A browser cannot guarantee prevention of:

- screenshots
- camera capture
- another device
- external notes
- accessibility tools
- operating-system level capture

The purpose is assessment integrity, not a claim of absolute prevention.

34. ASSESSMENT SCORING

There are exactly 30 scored questions.

15 position-specific
15 English

Passing grade:
85%

Whole-question scoring means:

30/30 = 100%
29/30 = 96.67%
28/30 = 93.33%
27/30 = 90%
26/30 = 86.67%
25/30 = 83.33%

PASS:

26 or more correct.

FAIL:

25 or fewer correct.

Official score must be calculated server-side.
