MOST RELEVANT Q&A

Q: What systems can be integrated? A: Depending on compatibility and
access, systems may include websites, applications, CRMs, ERPs,
databases, SaaS platforms, payment services, cloud services, and other
business applications.

Q: Why integrate systems? A: Integration can reduce duplicate work,
improve data flow, automate processes, and help different systems
operate as part of a coordinated environment.

Q: Can Operava integrate an old system with a new application? A:
Potentially, depending on the legacy system’s interfaces, data
structures, access methods, security requirements, and technical
constraints.

Q: Can integrations use APIs? A: Yes. APIs are commonly used where
supported and appropriate, along with other integration approaches when
required.

Q: Can integration be part of a larger software project? A: Yes.
Integration can be included as part of application development, platform
development, modernization, or digital transformation initiatives.

OPERAVA SERVICE MESSAGE Your systems should communicate with your
business—not work against it. Operava connects technology environments
so information can move through defined processes with less duplication
and greater operational coordination.
