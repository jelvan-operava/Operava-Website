MOST RELEVANT Q&A

Q: What does a help desk team actually handle? A: A help desk can
receive requests, create and update tickets, perform defined
troubleshooting, provide basic assistance, route issues, communicate
status, and escalate matters that require specialized intervention.

Q: Can Operava support internal employees? A: Yes. Help desk operations
can be designed around employee IT or service requests, subject to the
agreed scope and client systems.

Q: Can Operava work with our ticketing platform? A: Yes, where the
platform and access arrangement are compatible with the engagement.
Staff can follow the client’s ticketing and escalation workflow.

Q: Can we have coverage for different queues? A: Yes. Multiple teams can
be assigned to different queues, products, regions, user groups, or
support levels.

Q: What is the advantage over hiring one person internally? A: A managed
staffing model can provide access to broader operational capacity and
allow the business to scale beyond a single individual when demand
changes.
