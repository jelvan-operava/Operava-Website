MOST RELEVANT Q&A

Q: What is included in back-office operations? A: Depending on the
business, it can include order processing, account administration,
billing support, claims-related processing, records maintenance,
research, reporting, scheduling, data handling, and other defined
administrative workflows.

Q: Can Operava handle only one process? A: Yes. A business can outsource
a single process and later expand into additional functions or teams.

Q: Can back-office teams work alongside our internal staff? A: Yes. The
outsourced team can be structured as an operational extension of the
client’s existing organization.

Q: Is back-office outsourcing suitable for small businesses? A: Yes.
Smaller organizations can use one or a few specialists for functions
that do not justify building a full internal department.

Q: Can Operava support high-volume operations? A: Yes. Team size and
workflow design can be aligned with expected transaction volumes,
service requirements, and process complexity.

OPERAVA SERVICE MESSAGE Your internal team should spend more time moving
the business forward—not processing every repetitive task behind it.
Operava provides flexible back-office professionals who can become an
extension of your operational team.
