MOST RELEVANT Q&A

Q: What does data processing involve? A: It can involve collecting,
organizing, validating, updating, classifying, formatting, reconciling,
and preparing information according to defined business rules.

Q: Can Operava process large volumes of information? A: Yes. Staffing
can be structured around volume, turnaround requirements, process
complexity, and quality standards.

Q: Can data processing be combined with quality assurance? A: Yes. A
workflow can include processing professionals and quality reviewers to
support consistency and accuracy.

Q: Can Operava process industry-specific information? A: Yes, when the
required process, training, access, confidentiality, and compliance
requirements are properly defined.

Q: Can we start small? A: Yes. A single specialist can support a defined
process before the operation expands into a larger team.
