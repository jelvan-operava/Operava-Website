MOST RELEVANT Q&A

Q: What is SaaS development? A: SaaS development involves building
software delivered as an ongoing online service, commonly supporting
multiple users, accounts, permissions, subscriptions, integrations, and
recurring operations.

Q: Can Operava build a SaaS product from an idea? A: Yes. A project can
progress from requirements and product definition into architecture,
development, testing, deployment, and continued enhancement.

Q: Can SaaS platforms support multiple customers? A: Yes. Architecture
can be designed around the required customer, account, tenant, user,
permission, and data model.

Q: Can SaaS systems connect to third-party services? A: Yes. APIs and
approved integrations can connect the platform with external services
and business systems.

Q: Can an existing SaaS product be improved? A: Yes. Operava can support
feature development, modernization, integrations, performance
improvements, testing, and technical enhancements.
