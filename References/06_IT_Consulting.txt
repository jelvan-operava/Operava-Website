MOST RELEVANT Q&A

Q: What does IT consulting help with? A: It can support technology
assessment, system planning, modernization, automation, architecture,
digital transformation, integration strategy, implementation planning,
and other defined technology decisions.

Q: Does IT consulting require us to replace our current systems? A:
No. Consulting should evaluate the current environment first and
determine whether improvement, integration, modernization, replacement,
or another approach is appropriate.

Q: Can Operava help a small business plan its technology? A: Yes.
Consulting can be scaled to a specific business requirement rather than
requiring an enterprise-sized technology program.

Q: Can consulting lead into development or implementation? A: Yes. Where
appropriate, recommendations can be followed by software development,
systems integration, cloud services, or other technology services.

Q: What is the main benefit? A: It helps organizations make technology
decisions based on business requirements, technical considerations,
operational impact, and long-term objectives.
